Cortes Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Cortes Studio is a personal, self-hosted video tool operated by Guilherme Nini Veronezzi, an individual based in São José dos Campos, SP, Brazil ("I", "me"). It runs on my own computer and is used only by me to turn long videos into short vertical clips and publish them to YouTube and TikTok accounts that I own and connect to it. This policy explains what data Cortes Studio accesses and what happens to it.

Services Cortes Studio uses

  • YouTube API Services. Cortes Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to a YouTube channel that the account owner connects. By using Cortes Studio you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok APIs. Cortes Studio uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API to post videos to a TikTok account that the account owner connects. TikTok's Privacy Policy applies to data held by TikTok.

Data accessed, and why

When a YouTube channel is connected (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly):

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, to upload videos on the owner's behalf.
  • The channel ID and channel title, to confirm that the connected channel is the one configured for that profile and to show which channel will receive the upload.
  • The ID of each uploaded video, to show its status.

When a TikTok account is connected (scopes user.info.basic, user.info.profile, video.upload, video.publish):

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens and the account's open ID, to post on the owner's behalf.
  • The username and display name, to confirm the connected account and to show it on the posting screen.
  • Creator settings returned by TikTok (nickname, available privacy options, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, maximum video length), to build the posting screen. These are fetched when needed and not stored.
  • The publish ID of each post, to check its processing status.

Cortes Studio does not read, store or analyse other videos, comments, analytics, subscribers or followers of the connected accounts.

How the data is stored and shared

  • Everything is stored locally, in a database file on the computer that runs Cortes Studio. There is no Cortes Studio server, and no data is sent to me or to anyone else.
  • Data from the YouTube and TikTok APIs is sent only back to YouTube and TikTok, as part of the upload. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The videos being edited (not data from the YouTube or TikTok APIs) may be sent to the Google Gemini API for transcript and frame analysis, to pick moments and write titles.
  • Cortes Studio shows no ads and lets no third party serve content or ads.
  • The Cortes Studio dashboard stores interface preferences (for example, the last layout chosen) in the browser's local storage on that same computer. It uses no tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept while the account stays connected.
  • Channel and account names are refreshed when the token is refreshed and are never kept for more than 30 days without being refreshed.
  • Disconnecting an account inside Cortes Studio revokes its token with Google or TikTok immediately and deletes the stored tokens and account data. If access is revoked from the Google or TikTok side instead, Cortes Studio deletes the related data the next time it fails to use the token, and always within 30 days.
  • To request deletion of any data, write to veronezzi14@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 7 days.

Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: you can revoke Cortes Studio's access at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • TikTok: you can remove Cortes Studio from your account in the TikTok app settings, in the list of apps and services connected to your account.

Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or ask how it is used, by writing to the contact below.
